How it works (Workflow Diagram)

What it is: This acts as a structured reflection diary. It is designed to capture thoughts, experiences, or reflections over time from your study participants.

How it works: Participants submit text entries or markdown notes guided by a template you define. Entries can be submitted via the Web UI, a mobile application API, or the Telegram Bot.

What you can do: You can stream new diary reflections to an OOCSI channel or download the accumulated diary records as a CSV spreadsheet.
