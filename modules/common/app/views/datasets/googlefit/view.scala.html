How it works (Workflow Diagram)

What it is: This is a fitness and activity logger. It collects movement and physical metrics from the Google Fit app and connected wearables.

How it works: Participants authenticate with Google. Data Foundry periodically queries the Google Fit REST API to sync steps, activity, and weight.

What you can do: Send synced fitness logs via OOCSI or download them as a CSV spreadsheet.
